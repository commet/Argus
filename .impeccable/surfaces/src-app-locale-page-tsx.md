# Landing surface brief

The first viewport must make the product understandable without the Odyssey
metaphor. It promises one concrete loop: stuck decision → observable next move →
reality returns → next judgment. The one-line input is the only primary action.
The adjacent dark ledger is a clearly labeled product example, not user data or
a performance claim.

Keep the established paper, ink, brass, editorial type, and quiet animation.
Do not add a dashboard, feature-card grid, decorative glass, or another hero
claim. The mythology may deepen the world below the fold after utility is clear.
