# Argus Product Context

## Platform

- Primary surface: responsive web application.
- Companion surfaces: local and remote MCP servers plus an Agent Plugins-compatible package.
- The web app is the durable home for active decisions, returns, and accumulated learning. MCP captures and resumes decisions where work already happens. The plugin packages the same loop for agent hosts; it is not a separate product or methodology.

## Users

- Primary wedge: founders and product leaders making consequential product or market decisions with a single accountable owner, a real next action, and an observable signal within roughly 1–12 weeks.
- Secondary users: team leads and independent professionals facing recurring work decisions with meaningful opportunity cost.
- Users are busy, already use general AI, and do not want to learn a decision framework. They need one useful contribution quickly and a reason to return later.

## Product Purpose

Argus turns a consequential decision into a **decision-to-reality loop**: understand what is actually being decided, improve the weakest part of the thinking, choose a real next move, and return when reality can answer. Each completed loop preserves the user’s words and authorship, adds observations without rewriting the past, and can produce a user-approved lesson for the next decision.

The core job is not “give me a smarter answer.” It is:

1. Help me get unstuck and choose the next move now.
2. Keep the decision, its assumptions, and its return condition intact while I act.
3. Bring it back when something is knowable.
4. Help me carry one earned lesson into the next decision without scoring or profiling me.

## Positioning

- Category: closed-loop decision partner; user-facing shorthand is “결정에서 현실까지” / “from decision to reality.”
- Alternative: general AI chat, decision templates, reminder tools, and project-planning suites.
- Differentiation: the durable unit is a completed reality loop, not an answer, document, task list, or prediction score.
- Front-door promise: “결정을 다음 움직임으로. 현실이 답하면, 다음 판단까지.” / “Turn the decision into a next move. When reality answers, make the next decision better.”
- Product proof: a visible before → changed → adopted move → return contract → observation → next rule chain owned by the user.

## Operating Context

- The first session must provide a material contribution before asking for more context and should end in a user-adopted state: decide, test, research, defer, reframe, or stop.
- Important decisions are naturally low-frequency. Repeat use comes from a small portfolio of active loops, useful returns, and capture inside existing AI work—not from engagement prompts or manufactured daily activity.
- Local-first operation and optional account sync are core trust properties. A disconnected surface must say that it is disconnected.
- The current product includes substantial legacy strategy-workspace, multi-agent, document-review, and persona functionality. These are supporting tools only when they move one decision loop forward; they must not define the product’s front door.

## Capabilities and Constraints

### Required capabilities

- Preserve the user’s baseline wording, AI contribution provenance, user-adopted decision, next action, and return condition.
- Make one useful intervention at a time: mirror, reframe, research, alternative, trade-off, test, conditional recommendation, or stop.
- Maintain a small portfolio of active decisions and surface the most useful next action or due return.
- Collect observation before revealing or interpreting the old record during a return.
- Append outcomes and user-approved lessons without mutating history.
- Resume the same semantic case across web, MCP, and plugin surfaces.

### Constraints

- Never infer or save a user decision without an explicit adoption action.
- Never present AI proposals as the user’s original belief or later observations as model verdicts.
- Never score the person, decision quality, prediction accuracy, or “win rate.”
- Do not claim universal neutrality, correctness, automatic monitoring, or sync that has not occurred.
- Medical, legal, and financial expert responsibility is out of scope.
- The product should not require framework literacy, long onboarding, or a full project setup before the first useful result.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep judgment human while remaining actively useful.
- Remember exactly; do not polish the past.
- Notice beneath the surface without acting as an oracle.
- Watch only signals the user selected and name monitoring limits plainly.
- Return first on the promised date or material signal.
- Stay without judging; silence and “not yet” are valid outcomes.
- Use calm, direct, humane language. Product concepts should be explained in ordinary words before internal terms such as seal, settlement, ledger, voyage, or playbook appear.

## Evidence

### Observed

- Internal product planning reports that users value concrete prepare / research / execute plans with deadlines.
- The repository contains working web, MCP, plugin, return, receipt, notification, local-ledger, and cross-surface sync foundations.
- Current internal plans report no validated return-rate evidence yet; return value and repeat-use remain unproven.
- The current landing hero leads with judgment accumulation and philosophical differentiation, while the workspace still exposes a broad strategy-planning pipeline. The decision-to-reality loop is therefore harder to recognize than the underlying implementation warrants.

### Inferred

- The highest-leverage product change is to make “next move + promised return + earned next rule” the visible spine across the first screen, workspace home, MCP descriptions, and plugin entry skill.
- A small active-loop home can solve the frequency problem better than adding more tools, agents, dashboards, or content.
- The nautical and Argus mythology can deepen memory after the utility is understood, but should not carry comprehension on the first screen.

### Open decisions to validate with real use

- Whether “decision-to-reality loop” or a shorter phrase is the most immediately understood category language.
- Whether the primary first-session adoption is most often DECIDE, TEST, or RESEARCH for the product/market wedge.
- Whether portfolio returns create a genuinely useful weekly rhythm without becoming reminder noise.
- Whether the portable plugin should remain a broad Claude-oriented review suite or converge on one cross-host Argus loop skill plus optional host-specific extensions.

## Principles

1. Lead with the user’s next move, not Argus’s philosophy.
2. One screen, one primary action, one new cognitive demand.
3. Contribution before question; action before artifact; observation before retrospective interpretation.
4. Every durable record requires explicit user adoption and visible provenance.
5. Web, MCP, and plugin project the same loop; surface capability may differ, meaning may not.
6. Returns are the product’s proof. If users do not return and change a later decision, more first-session sophistication is not success.
7. Reduce visible machinery. Agents, methods, maps, receipts, and metaphors earn their place only by moving or closing a loop.
8. Prefer a small, trustworthy portfolio over a large dashboard.
9. Treat “not yet,” “stop,” and silence as honest states, not churn to manipulate.
10. Validate the loop with real behavior and preserve uncertainty in claims until evidence exists.

## Accessibility

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA for public and core product surfaces.
- All primary flows must work by keyboard, expose clear accessible names, retain visible focus, and preserve a logical reading order.
- Touch targets should be at least 44×44 CSS pixels for primary controls.
- Do not rely on color, motion, metaphor, or illustration alone to communicate state.
- Respect reduced motion and maintain legible text contrast in light and dark themes.
- Mobile hierarchy must put the active decision or due return before history and secondary navigation.
