# Argus Positioning - Living Course

Date: 2026-06-29

> Important decisions shouldn't end as answers. Argus keeps them alive as courses.

## Why This Is Stronger

This is not a shrink back to "find hidden assumptions." Hidden-assumption work is
the entry point, not the product boundary.

Argus's stronger claim is that a decision becomes a durable object with state:

- the original question,
- the sharpened question,
- the hidden assumptions,
- the alternatives and roads not taken,
- claims that can be checked or challenged,
- preserved state,
- and the later reality check.

Normal AI gives an answer. Argus keeps the decision alive long enough for
judgment, revision, memory, and reality to touch it.

## Relationship To The Voyage Metaphor

The voyage metaphor should remain, but it must serve the functional promise.

- Sirens: fluent AI agreement that sounds safe before it is checked.
- Binding: the user holds their own criteria before the model persuades them.
- Voyage: the decision moves through forks, checks, and revisions.
- Current Bearing: the honest course right now.
- Land / reality: the later moment when the world answers.

The hero should not ask users to admire the mythology first. It should make the
functional promise first, then let the myth deepen it.

## Product Implication

The webapp should feel like the place where living decisions are visible and
settled.

The plugin should feel like the place where a decision is captured at the moment
it appears in real work: a PR, repo, document, plan, or hard question.

Both surfaces should converge on the same truth:

> Argus is where important decisions stay alive until reality answers.
