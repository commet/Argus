---REPLY---
The pattern `/^\d{3}-\d{4}$/` matches strings that consist entirely of exactly three digits, followed by a literal hyphen, followed by exactly four digits — the classic US local phone number format like `555-1234`. Breaking it down: `^` anchors the match to the start of the string, `\d{3}` requires exactly three digit characters (0-9), `-` matches a literal hyphen, `\d{4}` requires exactly four digit characters, and `$` anchors the match to the end of the string. Because both anchors are present, the entire string must conform to this shape — no extra characters, spaces, or digits before or after are allowed, so `"123-4567"` matches, but `"1234567"` (no hyphen), `"12-3456"` (wrong grouping), `"123-45678"` (too many trailing digits), or `" 123-4567"` (leading space) would all fail to match.
