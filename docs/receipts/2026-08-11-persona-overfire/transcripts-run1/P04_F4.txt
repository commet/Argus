---REPLY---
This regex matches strings that consist of exactly 3 digits, followed by a hyphen, followed by exactly 4 digits — nothing before or after (that's what the `^` and `$` anchors enforce). So something like `123-4567` would match, but `1234-567`, `12-3456`, or `123-4567 ` (with trailing space) wouldn't. It's a pretty classic pattern for things like short local phone numbers or similar ID formats.
